About the project

Project Overview

MedSpark is a digital platform designed to connect people with medical assistance and provide opportunities to save money through a cashback system. Users could accumulate small amounts of money from purchases made at partner pharmacies, turning everyday purchases into savings.

How the cashback system worked

✦

For each eligible purchase at an affiliated pharmacy, users accumulated cents that could be saved over time.

✦

Partner pharmacies paid for the customer referrals generated through the platform.

Connecting People & Pharmacies

Pharmacies

Reach new customers through the platform.
