About the project

Project Overview

Routify is a digital platform designed to modernize traditional bus route management. It replaces time-consuming paper-based processes with a digital system that helps organize, automate and keep track of daily transportation operations.

Two ways to use Routify

User Access

Users can check their bus routes and know whether their bus is approaching or has already passed.

Administrator Access

Administrators can manage and keep track of daily bus route operations through a digital system.

Project objective

From Paper-Based Processes to Digital Management.

Routify aims to reduce manual work and make daily transportation management more organized and efficient. By digitalizing route records, the platform helps administrators manage information while giving users better visibility of their bus routes.
